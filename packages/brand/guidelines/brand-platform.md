# CAPSTONE brand platform

## Brand role

CAPSTONE is the expert ally between a protection need, a complex insurance market, and the moment a policy must work. It listens, compares, explains, and accompanies.

## Working positioning

For people, families, and businesses that want to protect what matters without navigating a complex market alone, CAPSTONE is the insurance advisor that turns complexity into clear decisions and remains present when it matters. Unlike a direct carrier relationship, CAPSTONE brings comparative perspective, plain-language counsel, and continuity across the life of the policy.

This positioning is strategic working language. “Broker,” “agency,” “advisor,” and any other regulated category term must be replaced with the exact Legal/Compliance-approved wording before publication.

## Value proposition

We listen to what you want to protect, compare the market for you, explain the options clearly, and stay with you from quotation through claim.

## Internal brand idea

**Claridad que sostiene.**

This is the organizing idea for decisions and experience, not a public tagline until formally approved. Clarity enables choice. Criteria organize. Accompaniment sustains. The gold keystone makes that relationship visible.

## Public verbal territory

- Primary existing promise: **Seguros sin complicaciones.**
- Existing campaign territory: **La nueva era de los seguros.**
- Recommended extension: **Claridad para elegir. Respaldo para seguir.**
- Method: **Escuchamos. Comparamos. Explicamos. Acompañamos.**

## Purpose

Make protection easier to understand, choose, and use.

## Ambition

Set a new standard for clear, human, and contemporary insurance brokerage in Ecuador.

## Brand pillars

1. **Clarity for decisions** — translate technical complexity without removing necessary precision.
2. **Choice with criteria** — compare relevant alternatives and make meaningful differences visible.
3. **Protection tailored to reality** — understand first; recommend second.
4. **Presence when it matters** — remain useful through renewals, reimbursements, and claims.
5. **Experience that evolves** — combine experience, technology, and a forward-looking operating mindset.

## Personality

- Expert, not distant.
- Clear, not simplistic.
- Protective, not paternalistic.
- Close, not overfamiliar.
- Agile, not rushed.
- Contemporary, not trend-driven.
- Confident, not grandiose.

## Archetypes

Primary: **Sage / Guide** — compares, translates, and enables informed decisions.  
Secondary: **Caregiver** — stays present when protection becomes necessary.

## Experience principles

1. **Show the next step.** Every interaction states what happens now, who owns it, and what comes next.
2. **Make differences visible.** Comparison is designed around coverage, limits, deductibles, exclusions, and price—not decorative tables.
3. **Use plain language with full truth.** Simplicity never hides conditions or uncertainty.
4. **Lead with the client’s need.** Credentials support the story; they do not replace it.
5. **Continue after the signature.** Service moments are brand moments.

## Identity rationale

The five-stone arch expresses a system whose strength comes from organization. The gold keystone represents CAPSTONE’s role: the piece that connects needs, options, criteria, decisions, and ongoing support. The lowercase wordmark balances structural confidence with digital fluency and approachability. Together, they avoid familiar shield, umbrella, and lock clichés while remaining immediately connected to protection and stability.
